CLINICS BY PROB N TECH: WEBSITE REQUIREMENTS
=============================================

Every requirement given for the marketing website so far, by area. Dates are
when it was asked for or decided (2026). This is a list of requirements, not
of npm packages (those are in package.json).


1. GENERAL
----------
- A marketing website for "Clinics by Prob N Tech", clinic software for
  Pakistani clinics: a live token queue, patient records and visit history,
  and prescriptions with Urdu instructions.
- Built with Next.js 16, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4, GSAP and Lenis.
- Pages: Home (/), Products (/products), Success stories (/success-stories),
  Pricing (/pricing), FAQ (/faq) and Contact (/contact). The navbar links
  to all six, in that order.
- Visual style: glass cards on a mint-teal background, the same on every page
  (pages redesigned to match the home page, 27 Oct).
- Light theme by default for everyone; dark mode must keep working, with a
  toggle in the navbar (22 Oct).
- Must work at phone (390px), laptop (1280px) and wide (1920px) widths:
  nothing scrolls sideways, nothing is cut off, buttons are easy to tap.
- Content must show without JavaScript and without animations. Animations
  may move content but never start it invisible. "Reduce motion" is
  respected everywhere.
- Smooth scrolling (Lenis) on the marketing pages, except with "reduce
  motion" and on touch, where the browser's own scroll is used (24 Oct).
- Sections below the hero load lazily but are always in the HTML (for search
  engines), and rise in once as they come into view (24 Oct).


2. WORDING AND CONTENT
----------------------
- Sentence case everywhere ("Book a free demo", not "Book A Free Demo").
- The demo button is always "Book a free demo"; the sign-in link is always
  "Sign in"; the brand is always "Clinics by Prob N Tech".
- The brand name never breaks across lines inside "Prob N Tech"
  (non-breaking spaces, 23 Oct).
- Say only what is true about the product: no invented features, numbers,
  customers or results. Example names and data are marked as examples.
- No em dashes or en dashes anywhere on the site. Use a hyphen for ranges
  ("2-5 doctors") and normal punctuation (comma, colon) in sentences
  (27 Oct). This applies to the chatbot's replies too.
- Urdu text is marked lang="ur" and dir="rtl" and set in Noto Nastaliq Urdu,
  about 1.1-1.15 times the size of the English next to it (24 Oct).
- Urdu translations of the FAQ questions are ours and need the owner's
  review.


3. HOME PAGE
------------
Order of sections: Hero, Specialties, How it works, FAQ, call-to-action
band, demo form, footer.
- Hero: a scroll story pinned on laptop and wider screens (24 Oct):
  1. the queue tablet and stethoscope;
  2. scene 1 fades away, four glass boxes rise into a 2x2 grid with a
     heartbeat line;
  3. each feature in turn, with copy and buttons on the left and a small
     app mock-up on the right.
  Nothing from one scene overlaps the next. Phones and tablets get no pin:
  the parts stack and fade in. The tablet floats, has parallax on wide
  screens and tilts slightly towards the mouse.
- The first hero feature, "Book appointment", was asked for by the owner
  (24 Oct). The app has no appointment booking yet, so its copy is a
  placeholder.
- Specialties: 25 example doctors as glass balls that drop into a stage,
  pile up, and can be dragged, thrown or moved with the arrow keys. Our own
  physics. With "reduce motion" they sit in rows (24 Oct; 25 balls since
  27 Oct).
- How it works: four glass step cards in a 2x2 grid with numbered orbs and
  two small app mock-ups with example data (24 Oct).
- Features (components/marketing/features.tsx): six glass cards, two with
  mock-ups tagged "Example", and a soft light that follows the mouse
  (24 Oct). Built, but not on the home page at the moment; the hero's
  feature scenes cover the same ground.
- FAQ: a glass accordion, one answer open at a time, the first open to
  begin with; Urdu question under the English, answer in English (left)
  and Urdu (right) (24 and 27 Oct).
- Call-to-action band just before the demo form; its button scrolls to the
  form instead of opening a pop-up (24 Oct).
- Signed-in users who open the home page go to their dashboard.


4. PRODUCTS PAGE (/products, 27 Oct)
------------------------------------
- Three products:
  - DMS, Doctor Management System: 1 doctor, 1 receptionist;
  - CMS, Clinic Management System: up to 10 doctors, unlimited receptionists;
  - ECMS, Enterprise Clinic Management System: 10+ doctors, unlimited
    receptionists.
- Every product has every feature in the compare table (owner, 27 Oct).
- A "How many doctors?" size picker (2-10 doctors etc.) highlights the
  matching card and table column. Nothing is sent or stored.
- No prices on this page; prices stay on /pricing.


5. PRICING PAGE (/pricing, 27 Oct)
----------------------------------
- Plans, as supplied by the owner:
  - Starter (for solo practitioners): PKR 2,999 a month; 1 doctor,
    1 receptionist;
  - Clinic (for clinics with a team): PKR 9,999 a month; up to 10 doctors,
    unlimited receptionists; marked as the popular plan;
  - Enterprise (for hospitals & chains): custom quote.
- Billing switch: Monthly (default) or Annually. Annually is 12 months for
  the price of 10 (2 months free). Only the prices change, in place, with no
  layout shift.
- Every plan starts with a 30-day free trial; we set up the clinic for the
  customer.
- Prices come from one place (lib/billing.ts), shared with the real app, the
  FAQ and the chatbot, so they never disagree.
- Every price and feature is visible without JavaScript.


6. SUCCESS STORIES PAGE (/success-stories, 27 Oct)
--------------------------------------------------
- Until clinics agree to be featured, every story is an illustrative sample
  and the page says so: no clinic names, quotes, numbers or results.
- Workflows are written as proposals to confirm in a demo, not as promised
  features.
- Filter by product and search, working together; a story opens in a
  dialog.
- Small front-facing sample mock-ups (never tilted), each tagged as a
  sample, with invented generic data.


7. FAQ PAGE (/faq, 27 Oct)
--------------------------
- Questions and answers as supplied by the owner, each in English and Urdu.
- Search (questions, English and Urdu answers, topic names) and topic list,
  working together.
- One answer open at a time; English on the left, Urdu on the right (one
  under the other on phones).
- Prices, the annual offer and plan limits come from lib/billing.ts.


8. CONTACT PAGE AND DEMO FORM

- Contact page form (27 Oct): name, clinic, phone and email are required

9. WHATSAPP AND CHAT DOCK (27 Oct)
----------------------------------
- One teal pill at the bottom right with "Ask Chatbot" and, after a thin
  divider, WhatsApp. It replaces the old separate WhatsApp button.
- Not shown on /contact (which lists both), and moves out of the way while a
  form field has focus.
- WhatsApp's own green (#25d366) is the one allowed fixed colour besides the
  three window dots.


10. CHATBOT ("Ask Chatbot", 27 Oct)
-----------------------------------
- Panel: glass panel above the dock (a sheet from the bottom on phones);
  header with the name, an "AI assistant" badge, "Talk to a person"
  (WhatsApp), New chat and Close.
- First screen: a greeting by the visitor's time of day, suggested
  questions, and a "Prefer a person?" row with WhatsApp and demo links.
- A friendly robot mascot drawn in the site's teal.
- Answers only from the site's own data (plans, prices, FAQ, products,
  contact). Never invents prices, features, dates or results; gives no
  medical advice.
- Uses Google Gemini from the server only; the API key never reaches the
  browser (GEMINI_API_KEY in .env.local).
- Replies in Urdu script only when the question is in Urdu script;
  otherwise (English or Roman Urdu) in English.
- Never asks for or sends patient data: messages that look like patient
  details are answered on the server and never sent to Google. Nothing the
  visitor writes is logged or stored.
- Greetings ("hi", "salam", "good morning") and acknowledgements ("ok",
  "thanks", "theek hai", "shukriya") are answered on the server, instantly.
- Short questions (up to 4 words) that match a known topic ("price?",
  "who u", "me baat krun?") get the site's own answer instantly, without
  waiting for Gemini.
- Each new message is sent with only the previous exchange: the visitor's
  last question and the bot's answer (3 messages in all).
- If Gemini is slow (5 seconds) or busy, a second request races the first;
  the whole wait is capped at 15 seconds. On failure the bot gives the site's
  own answer if one matches, otherwise a clear error with "Try again",
  WhatsApp and Contact.
- About 10 messages a minute per visitor.
- Replies are plain text; only the site's own page paths (/pricing, etc.)
  become links; any PKR amount must be one the site states.
- Works without a Gemini key, from the site's own answers.


11. ACCESSIBILITY
-----------------
- Every image has alt text; every field has a label.
- Everything works with the keyboard (accordions, dialogs, picker, chat).
- Text contrast at least 4.5:1 in light and dark mode.
- Screen readers hear each chatbot answer once, whole.


12. RULES FOR CHANGES
---------------------
- Colours only from the design tokens (ui/src/styles/globals.css); no hex,
  rgb or hsl in components, except the WhatsApp green and the window dots.
- Ask before adding a package to package.json.
- Don't change the STAND-IN files or the shared ui/src/components beyond
  what a page needs.
- Before sending work back: npm run typecheck and npm run build must pass.
